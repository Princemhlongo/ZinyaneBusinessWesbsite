OUR STORY

Who We Are

Founded in 2021, Black Linear Studios — operating under the brand Ideal Media Content (IMC) — was built from a passion for visual storytelling and a belief that every moment, every event, and every brand deserves to be captured with the same cinematic care given to major productions.

Based in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, we work across South Africa — shooting weddings, traditional ceremonies, music videos, brand commercials, and events of every scale. Our work has taken us from intimate family gatherings to commercial productions for brands like Savanna and Corona.

We are not just photographers and videographers. We are directors. We don't just show up and press record — we shape the narrative, control the light, and deliver work that lasts.
